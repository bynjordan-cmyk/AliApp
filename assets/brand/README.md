# Recursos de marca AliApp

Origen: lámina de identidad proporcionada por la usuaria el 27 de septiembre de 2026.
Los PNG se prepararon con la herramienta integrada image_gen a partir de esa referencia; son derivados raster, no los archivos vectoriales originales.

- aliapp-logo.png: composición horizontal usada por BrandLogo (cabeceras y acceso).
- aliapp-mark.png: símbolo para el favicon web.

Prompts utilizados:

1. Extract the approved AliApp horizontal logo from the supplied brand board as a single clean production PNG on a genuinely transparent background. Preserve faithfully the large top coral A symbol with spoon-shaped white negative space and coral heart inside, followed by the exact rounded wordmark AliApp (Ali navy #0F2D5B, App coral #FF6B6B, coral heart above i). Preserve original proportions and shapes. No redesign. Remove ALL slogans, taglines, other icons, color swatches, board layout and annotations. Only symbol plus wordmark. Tight horizontal canvas with small even transparent padding, crisp flat edges, no shadow, no texture. Asset for app header.

2. Extract only the approved AliApp ICON ONLY mark from this brand board into a square production PNG: coral #FF6B6B rounded A with white spoon negative space and coral heart in spoon. Preserve the exact design. Center on pure white background, occupy 80% of square. No text, no additional elements, no shadow, no frame. Crisp clean app favicon asset.
